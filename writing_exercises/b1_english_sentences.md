# B1 German Writing Exercises - English Sentences

Translate the following sentences into German. These exercises mix various B1 grammar topics including genitive case, Präteritum, infinitive with zu, lassen, temporal prepositions/clauses, passive voice, conditional clauses, and Konjunktiv II.

---

## Sentences 1-10: Genitive Case & Präteritum

1. The color of the car is blue.
2. I was very tired yesterday.
3. She had no time for the meeting.
4. The roof of the house is old.
5. We were in Berlin last year.
6. Because of the weather, we stayed at home.
7. During the vacation, he read many books.
8. The students' answers were correct.
9. She was able to solve the problem.
10. The price of the tickets was too high.

---

## Sentences 11-20: Infinitive with "zu" & Lassen

11. I hope to travel to Italy next summer.
12. It is important to learn German.
13. She decided to study medicine.
14. I have no time to cook today.
15. He is planning to buy a new computer.
16. I am having my car repaired.
17. Let us go to the cinema together!
18. She is having her hair cut tomorrow.
19. It is possible to finish the work today.
20. I forgot to call my mother.

---

## Sentences 21-30: Temporal Prepositions & Temporal Clauses

21. I have been living here since 2020.
22. After I had finished my homework, I watched TV.
23. Before you leave, please close the window.
24. While she was cooking, he was reading the newspaper.
25. We will wait until the rain stops.
26. When I was a child, I played soccer every day.
27. As soon as I arrive home, I will call you.
28. Starting from next Monday, the shop will be closed.
29. Within two weeks, you will receive the package.
30. At Christmas, we always visit our grandparents.

---

## Sentences 31-40: Passive Voice & Consecutive Clauses

31. The house was built in 1995.
32. German is spoken in Austria.
33. The letter will be sent tomorrow.
34. The car is being repaired by the mechanic.
35. The problem has been solved.
36. She spoke so quietly that I couldn't understand her.
37. It was so cold that the lake froze.
38. He ran so fast that he won the race.
39. The children were picked up by their parents.
40. The book must be returned to the library.

## Sentences 41-50: Conditional & Causal Clauses, Konjunktiv II

41. If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
42. Because I was sick, I didn't go to work.
43. If I had more money, I would travel around the world.
44. She didn't come to the party because she had to work.
45. I would be happy if you could help me.
46. If I were you, I would study harder.
47. You should eat more vegetables.
48. We would have more time if we started earlier.
49. Could you please help me with this exercise?
50. I wish I could speak German fluently.
