## How to Use These Exercises

### For Maximum Learning Benefit:

1. **Start with A2 Level** (if you need to review basics)
   - Open `a2_english_sentences.md`
   - Try to translate each sentence into German
   - Write your answers in a notebook or document
   - Check your answers against `a2_german_answers.md`
   - Read the grammar notes carefully for each section

2. **Progress to B1 Level**
   - Open `b1_english_sentences.md`
   - Translate sentences without looking at answers
   - Focus on applying the specific grammar rules being tested
   - Compare with `b1_german_answers.md`
   - Study the detailed explanations provided

3. **Review Weak Areas**
   - If you struggle with specific grammar topics, revisit the corresponding week lessons
   - Practice those sentence types multiple times
   - Create your own similar sentences

## Grammar Topics Covered

### A2 Level (50 sentences)
- **Possessive Articles** - Declension across all cases
- **Perfect Tense** - haben vs. sein usage
- **Connectors** - deshalb, trotzdem, obwohl, dass, ob
- **Verbs with Prepositions** - Accusative and Dative combinations
- **Reflexive Verbs** - Daily routines and actions
- **Ordinal Numbers & Dates** - Expressing dates and ordinal positions
- **Relative Clauses** - Using der/die/das/dem/den/deren
- **Adjective Declension** - Weak, mixed, and strong declension
- **Two-Way Prepositions** - Wo? (Dative) vs. Wohin? (Accusative)

### B1 Level (50 sentences)
- **Genitive Case** - Possession and genitive prepositions
- **Präteritum** - Simple past tense (especially sein, haben, modals)
- **Infinitive with zu** - After verbs, adjectives, and nouns
- **Lassen Verb** - Having things done, suggestions, permissions
- **Temporal Prepositions** - seit, ab, während, innerhalb, an, in
- **Temporal Subordinate Clauses** - nachdem, bevor, während, als, wenn, sobald, bis
- **Consecutive Clauses** - so dass / so...dass (expressing results)
- **Passive Voice** - All tenses with werden + Partizip II
- **Conditional Clauses** - wenn clauses (real and hypothetical)
- **Causal Clauses** - weil (because)
- **Konjunktiv II** - Hypothetical situations, wishes, polite requests, advice

## Study Tips

### Before Your B1 Exam:

1. **Complete all 100 sentences** (50 A2 + 50 B1) at least once
2. **Redo difficult sentences** - Mark sentences you got wrong and practice them again
3. **Time yourself** - Try to translate sentences within 1-2 minutes each
4. **Focus on accuracy** - Pay attention to:
   - Correct case usage (Nominativ, Akkusativ, Dativ, Genitiv)
   - Verb conjugation and position
   - Adjective endings
   - Preposition + case combinations
   - Word order in subordinate clauses

5. **Create variations** - Once you master a sentence, try changing it:
   - Change the subject (ich → wir → sie)
   - Change the tense (Präsens → Perfekt → Präteritum)
   - Add additional clauses
   - Change gender of nouns

6. **Review grammar notes** - The answer files contain detailed explanations - don't skip them!

7. **Mix and match** - Real exam questions combine multiple grammar topics, just like these sentences do
