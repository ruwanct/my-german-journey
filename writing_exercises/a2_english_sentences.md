# A2 German Writing Exercises - English Sentences

Translate the following sentences into German. These exercises mix various A2 grammar topics including possessive articles, perfect tense, connectors, verbs with prepositions, reflexive verbs, relative clauses, adjective declension, and two-way prepositions.

---

## Sentences 1-10: Possessive Articles & Perfect Tense

1. My brother has bought a new car.
2. Have you (informal) visited your grandparents?
3. Her daughter has traveled to Spain.
4. We have eaten our breakfast already.
5. His friends have arrived at the airport.
6. Their house is bigger than our house.
7. Your (formal) appointment has been confirmed.
8. My parents have lived in Berlin for five years.
9. Has she forgotten her keys at home?
10. Our teacher has explained the lesson very well.

---

## Sentences 11-20: Connectors & Word Order

11. I am tired, therefore I am going to bed early.
12. She wants to come to the party, but she has no time.
13. Although it is raining, we are going for a walk.
14. He says that he will arrive tomorrow.
15. I don't know whether he is coming to the meeting.
16. Nevertheless, I continued working on the project.
17. That's why I couldn't call you yesterday.
18. Even though I studied a lot, the exam was difficult.
19. Do you know when the train departs?
20. I'm asking where the nearest supermarket is.
