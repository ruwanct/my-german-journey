🎯 TELC B1 Exam Practice - Sprachbausteine

B1 Week 10: Master Grammar Through Exam-Style Practice

📋 What are Sprachbausteine?

Sprachbausteine (Language Building Blocks) are grammar-focused cloze tests in the TELC B1 exam. They test your ability to use correct grammar in context.

Format:

  • Teil 1: Read a text with 10 gaps (21-30). Choose the correct answer from 3 options (a, b, c)
  • Teil 2: Read a text with 10 gaps (31-40). Fill each gap with the correct word from 15 given options
  • Time: Approximately 20 minutes for both parts

🎯 Focus Areas for This Practice:

Based on common B1 grammar challenges, we'll practice:

  • Adjektivdeklination - Adjective declension (most common errors!)
  • Relativsätze - Relative clauses (der, die, das, dem, den, deren)
  • Präpositionen - Prepositions with correct cases
  • Konjunktionen - Conjunctions (wenn, weil, obwohl, damit, dass, trotzdem)

📚 Quick Grammar Review

1. Adjective Declension Patterns

After der/die/das (definite):

  • Nominativ: der neue Film
  • Akkusativ: den neuen Film
  • Dativ: dem neuen Film
  • Genitiv: des neuen Films

After ein/mein (indefinite):

  • Nominativ: ein neuer Film (m)
  • Akkusativ: einen neuen Film (m)
  • Dativ: einem neuen Film

No article (null):

  • Nominativ: neuer Film (m)
  • Akkusativ: neuen Film (m)
  • Dativ: neuem Film

2. Relative Pronouns

Mask. Fem. Neut. Plural
Nom. der die das die
Akk. den die das die
Dat. dem der dem denen
Gen. dessen deren dessen deren

3. Prepositions with Cases

Akkusativ: für, durch, gegen, ohne, um, bis

Dativ: aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu, außer, gegenüber

Wechselpräpositionen: an, auf, hinter, in, neben, über, unter, vor, zwischen

→ Wohin? (motion) = Akk. / Wo? (location) = Dat.

4. Important Conjunctions

Subordinating (verb at end):

  • wenn - if, when, whenever
  • weil - because
  • obwohl - although
  • damit - so that
  • dass - that

Coordinating (no word order change):

  • aber - but
  • trotzdem - nevertheless

✅ Answer Key & Explanations

Practice Test 1 - Answers:

21. b) meiner - "zu meiner Party" (Dativ, feminine: die Party → zu meiner Party)

22. a) ob - Indirect question "whether/if" (ob introduces yes/no questions)

23. b) das - Relative pronoun referring to "Team" (das Team → das besteht)

24. c) offiziellen - Adjective after possessive article (meinen ersten offiziellen Tag - Akkusativ masculine)

25. a) am - "am Computer arbeiten" (fixed expression: an + dem = am)

26. b) der - "in der Nähe" (fixed expression, feminine: die Nähe)

27. c) dem - "mit dem Fahrrad" (Dativ after "mit", neuter: das Fahrrad)

28. a) Obwohl - "Although it's stressful, I enjoy it" (contrast: obwohl = although)

29. b) die - Relative pronoun referring to "Projekte" (plural: die Projekte → die sind)

30. a) damit - "so that we can talk" (purpose: damit + we can talk = Nebensatz)

Practice Test 2 - Answers:

31. a) tolles - Adjective without article (neuter: ein tolles Hotel - Akkusativ)

32. b) das - Relative pronoun (neuter: das Hotel → das liegt)

33. a) gute - Adjective after "viele" (plural: viele gute Bewertungen)

34. a) wo - "where one can eat" (location: wo = where in relative clause)

35. b) für - "für zwei Wochen" (duration: für + time period, Akkusativ)

36. c) wenn - "if you also want to" (conditional: wenn = if)

37. a) über - "sich freuen über" (fixed: über + Akkusativ = about)

38. b) deren - Genitive relative pronoun (die Airline → deren Preise = whose prices)

39. a) Weil - "Because it's only a short flight" (reason: weil = because)

40. b) damit - "so that I can make the booking" (purpose: damit = so that)

Practice Test 3 - Answers:

41. a) IN - "in einer ruhigen Straße" (Dativ after "in" for location)

42. i) NEUEN - "eines neuen Hauses" (Genitiv: eines neuen Hauses)

43. c) MIT - "mit Südausrichtung" (with southern orientation)

44. d) DAS - Relative pronoun (das Wohnzimmer → das ist hell)

45. e) LETZTES - "letztes Jahr" (last year, no article → letztes)

46. b) DER - "in der Nähe" (fixed expression, Dativ: die Nähe)

47. j) SODASS - "so that one can travel" (consequence: sodass = so that)

48. k) PLUS - "plus Nebenkosten" (plus additional costs)

49. f) WENN - "Wenn Sie Interesse haben" (If you are interested - conditional)

📊 Common Mistakes & Tips

⚠️ Most Common Errors in Sprachbausteine:

  1. Adjective Declension
    • Forgetting to change endings after different articles
    • Mixing up masculine/neuter in Nominativ (neue vs. neuer)
    • Tip: Learn the three patterns: definite, indefinite, null article
  2. Relative Pronouns
    • Not matching gender/number of the noun they refer to
    • Wrong case (check what role it plays in the relative clause!)
    • Tip: Find the noun → determine gender → check function in clause
  3. Prepositions
    • Using wrong case after preposition
    • Not recognizing fixed expressions (sich freuen auf, sich interessieren für)
    • Tip: Memorize preposition + case combinations and verb + preposition pairs
  4. Conjunctions
    • Confusing ob (whether) with wenn (if/when)
    • Using damit (purpose) instead of dass (that)
    • Not checking if meaning requires contrast (obwohl) or reason (weil)
    • Tip: Read the whole sentence to understand the logical relationship

💡 Exam Strategy Tips:

  • Step 1: Read the whole text first to understand context
  • Step 2: Solve easy gaps first (conjunctions, prepositions in fixed expressions)
  • Step 3: For difficult gaps, identify: What part of speech is needed? What case?
  • Step 4: Mark uncertain answers and review them at the end
  • Step 5: Use elimination - cross out obviously wrong answers
  • Time management: Don't spend more than 1-2 minutes per gap
  • Always answer: No points deducted for wrong answers - always guess if unsure!
